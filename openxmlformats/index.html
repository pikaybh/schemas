Open XML Formats

This is an informational, historical entrypoint maintained for compatibility with early links into this schema registry. It is not assigned to any product or project.

Use versioned project namespaces instead. New XML vocabularies are published under /schemas/ns/<project>/<vocabulary>/<version>/ after a project reservation and specification review.

The registry is open to distinct projects and schema technologies, and does not belong to BIXML, Xim, or another single application. No schema is published at this unversioned location.
